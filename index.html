Trademark footprint appears

Separate public trademark records show marks including BIOPREP and VARIMET associated with entities using the Alomed name. The available records use a Prayagraj / Allahabad address and therefore should not automatically be treated as records of the Hyderabad legal entity.

Strike-off notice published

The Ministry of Corporate Affairs published the company in a Hyderabad-related strike-off notice/list under section 248(5). This is a significant corporate-status event and should be considered when interpreting historical product listings.
